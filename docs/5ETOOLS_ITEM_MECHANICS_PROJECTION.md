# a8.10.3 — Equipment and magic item mechanics

Rebuilds remaining-family evidence and binds physical items and fingerprints. Re-derives converted cost/weight from the retained record, preserves missing prices and accepts only exact integer copper and finite nonnegative weight. Quantity preview requires explicit context, both unit values and no bundle declaration; it is not a purchase or encumbrance calculation. Bundle contents and capacity remain pending.

Magic variants recheck the existing template selector and expansion against retained base/template records; a same-edition emitted base endpoint is separately qualified. No interpolation or effect execution. Explicit attunement true/false remains distinct from absent/conditional declarations and current state. Slot capacity preview uses caller-confirmed limits and other requirements, not a hardcoded maximum. Weapon and AC bonus values remain isolated; separate attack/damage bonuses, stacking and application are pending. Concrete rarity is descriptive only.

Charges: literal cap, explicit dawn/dusk/midnight event and fixed or bounded simple recovery dice are separate observations. No equivalence to rests, recovery application, shared pool or depletion interpretation. Charge preview only subtracts a caller-confirmed amount from a caller-confirmed instance/pool and current value. Attached spells require explicit source and same-edition emitted endpoint; class-feature grants and narrative effects remain pending.

No inventory mutation, runtime activation, new workflows, dependencies or upstream clones. Aggregate audit only. Next a8.10.4: remaining-family closure and isolated inventory/encounter preservation, then runtime-only contract preservation.
