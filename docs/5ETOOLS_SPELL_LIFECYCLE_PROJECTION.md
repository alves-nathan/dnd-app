## Implementation
- spell-lifecycle-projection.js recomputes spell parity evidence, binds each physical spell and source fingerprint, and reuses compileDuration for all 854 emitted spells (572 finite timed, 253 instantaneous, 26 termination pending, 3 indeterminate). Concentration is recorded only if source duration and projected field agree. Instantaneous and permanent/ambiguous durations are not converted into persistent finite groups.
- Hypothetical complete inventory of finite spell cast groups, explicit caster, cast and recipient effect identities, source fingerprint, rules edition, unit and remaining time. Unique cast/effect IDs and recipient per group; at most one active concentration per caster, not one per recipient. A start requires confirmed completion, stacking, recipient membership and ability to maintain; replacement requires explicit reviewed timing boundary. Ending concentration changes the whole cast group, preserves other casters, nonconcentration effects and ended/expired groups. No inferred exception for additional concentration.
- Elapsed time supplied explicitly in original duration unit, scoped by cast ID; no default turn boundary, conversion from rest, minute-to-round assumptions, or automatic duration tick. Termination requires explicit policy. Permanent and alternative duration semantics remain pending.
- Concentration DC uses damage actually taken, one confirmed damage event, Constitution save with floor(half damage), minimum 10; maximum 30 only in 2024. Zero damage has no check. Save total is supplied and confirmed (not inferred from natural dice, ability or modifiers); failed save ends group, success preserves. Confirmed death, incapacitation or voluntary drop end concentration. External save uses explicit confirmed DC. Damage aggregation, temporary HP handling, War Caster, conditions and exceptions remain externally reviewed.
- Rule references (paraphrased): 2014 Basic Rules spellcasting https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/spellcasting ; 2024 Basic Rules glossary https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary/ . The 2024 replacement starts during casting, not necessarily at completion; this preview does not model an interrupted cast and therefore requires a confirmed boundary. Long casting/ritual concentration, non-spell and unknown-duration effects, special dispel/termination rules, partial recipient removal, duration overrides and actual dependency/effect resolution are not supported. Inventory of only finite source-bound spell groups is not proof of complete live character inventory.
- Existing activeEffects.ts uses character-local concentration, default rest expiry and turn/rest ticks; no production changes. Six synthetic tests plus real aggregate audit. No extra dependencies, clone or workflow. Full CI pending; no full local run. No effect, HP, slot, inventory or character write.
