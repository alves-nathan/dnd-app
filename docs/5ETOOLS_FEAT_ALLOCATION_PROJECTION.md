# a8.9.3 — Feat ASI and instance projections

Rebuilds and binds the a8.9.2 requirement report, then re-derives the retained source ability plan and simple payload projection. Alternatives remain distinct. Fixed positive increases and explicit distinct choices can be previewed with complete six ability scores, exact alternative and confirmed score/allocation context. The cap is checked without truncating increases. Hidden, weighted, malformed and overlapping fixed/choice alternatives remain pending. The legacy name/options ASI fallback is not a source-qualified rule; absence of an ability declaration is not proof of absence of effects.

Repeatability is explicit true, false, undeclared, malformed or conditional pending. Notes, hidden flags and known legacy distinct-choice conditions cannot be treated as unconditional. Instance preview requires a confirmed complete inventory of same-edition source-fingerprinted physical feats, unique instance IDs and per-feat indices, and an explicitly confirmed physical-identity scope. It previews only the next index above the highest current index; no renumbering or removal. Historical deleted indices are not inferred, and the caller must explicitly confirm that the proposed ID/index does not conflict with retained history. Repeating requires explicit true and confirmation that other conditions are excluded. An empty inventory permits a first-instance candidate only, not acquisition. No name-based duplicate resolution or cross-edition migration.

Both previews are independent, pure and non-authoritative: prerequisites, choices, ASI, repeatability and grants must not be conflated into an acquisition decision. No live character inventory or write. No new workflow, dependency or upstream clone; only aggregate counts and fingerprint logged. Next a8.9.4: qualified choices, grants and resource evidence.
